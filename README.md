## Verification notes

- Every story was verified as having happened, or first been
  reported, on Thursday 26 November 2026, from a named major outlet.
  Full evidence per story is in `RESEARCH.md`.

- Every illustrated story carries a verification tag — CONFIRMED (two
  or more outlets), OFFICIAL (on the record), REPORTED (one outlet) —
  explained in the footer legend. Disputed figures are shown as claims,
  never as fact. The footer reads: "Claims are attributed, not stated
  as fact."

- Several near-misses were caught in research and excluded: the Cairo
  fire, the Hormuz tanker mines and the US tariff proposal are
  Wednesday's stories (25 Nov entry heroes) — Thursday carries only
  their follow-ups (the driver's arrest, the Kuwait attack, the
  tariff pushback); Wednesday night's Kyiv strike is excluded, only
  Zelenskyy's warning of a new attack is used; the FedEx Miami
  overrun and the Sydney runway scare happened Wednesday; the Canary
  record arrivals were Tuesday.

- `draw.py` prints no OVERFLOW warnings; counts reconcile
  (3 + 10 + 22 = 35; regions sum 35; 15 fields). `entry.png` is
  1024×1024.
