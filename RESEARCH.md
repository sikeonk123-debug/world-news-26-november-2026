## Heroes

1. **Gaza strikes kill 9 — MIDDLE EAST / GAZA — Reuters, AP.**
   Israeli airstrikes and gunfire killed at least 9 Palestinians in
   Gaza overnight, medics said, in a third consecutive night of air
   attacks despite a one-year-old ceasefire. A dawn strike on an
   apartment in Deir al-Balah killed Khaled Abu Nada, the Hamas
   deputy education minister, and his wife; two other strikes killed
   6 in Tuffah and Beach camp, and gunfire killed a 62-year-old man
   in Bureij later Thursday. Abu Nada was the third senior Hamas
   official killed since Tuesday. Tag CONFIRMED (Reuters medics + AP).
   Banner: 9 KILLED IN GAZA.

2. **Kuwait drones kill 2 Italians — MIDDLE EAST / KUWAIT —
   Reuters, BBC.** Iranian drones hit an air-defence surveillance
   station in Kuwait at about 14:00 local on Thursday, killing two
   Italian technicians working for Rheinmetall Italia (ages 36 and
   65); a Kuwaiti and a Pakistani national were also wounded by
   falling debris as Kuwaiti defences intercepted the drones. Italy
   and Kuwait blame Iran; Iran denies any role. Tag CONFIRMED
   (Italy + Kuwait blame Iran; Iran denies). Banner: 2 ITALIANS
   KILLED.

3. **Ebola passes 12,000 cases — AFRICA / DR CONGO — AP, Reuters.**
   DR Congo's Ebola outbreak topped 12,000 confirmed cases,
   government data showed Thursday: 12,021 cases, 5,904 dead. The
   fastest-growing Ebola outbreak on record and the second-largest;
   caused by the rare Bundibugyo strain, with no approved vaccine or
   treatment. Fighting resumed Thursday morning in Iga-Barrière,
   disrupting responders; the outbreak has spread to 8 of 26
   provinces. Tag CONFIRMED (AP + Reuters). Banner: 12,021 CASES ·
   5,904 DEAD.
